# Ikogosi Warm Springs

A site where a naturally occurring warm spring and a cold spring flow from separate sources on adjoining hills and meet in one pool, each reportedly keeping its own temperature. A resort and conference facility has operated on the hill above the springs since the site was developed for tourism.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Long treated as one of Ekiti's signature natural landmarks; oral tradition explains the two springs as a quarrelling husband and wife turned to water, while locals also attribute therapeutic properties to the warm spring. A Baptist mission ran a youth centre here from the early 1950s before the site was taken over for state tourism development in 1978.
