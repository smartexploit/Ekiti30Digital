# Arinta Waterfall

A multi-tier waterfall (locals report up to six/seven falls) cascading roughly 50 metres down rocky hills into a forest pool; about a 15-minute drive from Ikogosi. The surrounding area is a Ramsar-listed wetland site.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Community elders date the falls' existence to at least the 17th century and attribute healing properties to the water. Registered as Ramsar Site No. 2565 (Nigeria) for its wetland/biodiversity value, and long promoted by the state as a candidate for UNESCO heritage recognition.
