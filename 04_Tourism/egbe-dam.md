# Egbe Dam

A dam on the Ose River at Egbe-Ekiti, supplying pipe-borne water to the local government area and supporting fishing and irrigation farming; smoked and fresh fish from the area are sold across the state.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Presented by the state government as a resort-type tourist site alongside Ero Dam, illustrating how Ekiti's water infrastructure doubles as a leisure/tourism draw.
