# Erin Ayonigba Sacred Fish River

A river in Erinjiyan-Ekiti home to a colony of catfish regarded as sacred; the fish are not eaten and killing or eating them is traditionally forbidden.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

One of several Ekiti sites where traditional taboo protects local wildlife, similar to other Yoruba "sacred fish" rivers found elsewhere in southwestern Nigeria.
