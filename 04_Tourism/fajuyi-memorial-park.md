# Fajuyi Memorial Park

A triangular park in the centre of Ado-Ekiti built around the burial site of Lt. Col. Francis Adekunle Fajuyi, former military governor of Nigeria's old Western Region.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Col. Fajuyi was killed alongside Head of State Gen. Aguiyi-Ironsi in the July 1966 counter-coup; he was given a heroic burial for refusing to abandon his guest to the coup plotters. The park is now a memorial and recreational landmark, and hosts the 'Great Fajuyi Hall' built as part of Udiroko festival development projects.
