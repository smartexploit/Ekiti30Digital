# Abanijorin Rocks & Cave

## Documented Facts

A rock formation containing a cave, at the foot of a mountain along the Ado-Ekiti road in Iyin-Ekiti, locally known as "Okuta Aba ni Jorin" (Abanijorin Mountain). An independent 2024 news report quotes the Ekiti State Bureau of Tourism Development's Director-General describing the site as having seven caves, a rock tunnel, and an amphitheatre with a "breathtaking vista," and records the Ekiti governor and Nigeria's Minister of Tourism visiting the nearby palace of the Oluyin of Iyin-Ekiti as part of a state tourism-promotion trip.
