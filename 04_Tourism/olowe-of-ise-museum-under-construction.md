# Olowe of Ise Museum (under construction)

A museum announced by the Ekiti State Government to be built on the original compound of master carver Olowe of Ise, intended to house his works (many currently abroad), alongside educational programmes and exhibitions on Yoruba art.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Backed by a dedicated Olowe of Ise Arts Foundation (chaired by Senator Babafemi Ojudu) with a 13-member Board of Trustees including prominent Nigerian art figures (Yemisi Shyllon of the Shyllon Museum, Nike Okundaye of Nike Art Gallery, curator Tunde Odunlade); the National Commission for Museums and Monuments has also backed the preservation effort. A Lagos fundraiser was held to finance construction, artwork acquisition and running costs. STATUS: project in progress, not yet operational as of this collection — reconfirm before publishing as an existing museum.
