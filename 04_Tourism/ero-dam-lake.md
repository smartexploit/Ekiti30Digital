# Ero Dam & Lake

A man-made dam and lake on a river near Ikun-Ekiti; originally designed to cover about 5 km but grew to roughly 11 km, it supplies pipe-borne water to Oye, Ido-Osi and Moba local government areas.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

One of two state-highlighted dam/lake sites (with Egbe Dam) presented as combined infrastructure and tourism assets, supporting fishing (by canoe and motorised boat) and irrigation farming for surrounding communities.
