# Ewi of Ado-Ekiti Palace

The royal palace of the Ewi (paramount traditional ruler) of Ado-Ekiti, described as an architectural showcase of Ekiti royal history and regularly cited as a de facto mini-museum of artefacts and regalia.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Ado-Ekiti's status as capital of Ekiti State gives the Ewi's throne particular prominence; the palace is the venue for the annual Udiroko festival and other state ceremonies, and its grounds have seen recent additions such as a modern architectural amphitheatre.
