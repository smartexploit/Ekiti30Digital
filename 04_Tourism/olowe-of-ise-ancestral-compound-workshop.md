# Olowe of Ise Ancestral Compound & Workshop

The house and original workshop of master woodcarver Olowe of Ise (d. 1934) in Ise-Ekiti, which the Ekiti State Government has designated as the site of a planned museum.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Olowe of Ise is one of the most celebrated Yoruba sculptors of the 20th century, with carved veranda posts, doors and bowls held in major Western museums; the state government has cited the scattering of his work abroad as motivation for building a museum on his original compound.
