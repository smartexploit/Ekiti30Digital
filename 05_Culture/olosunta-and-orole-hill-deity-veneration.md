# Olosunta and Orole Hill Deity Veneration

The twin hills overlooking Ikere-Ekiti, Olosunta and Orole, are each treated as the seat of a resident deity; worshippers credit the deities with granting children to childless women and protecting the town in past wars.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Ikere-Ekiti is traditionally described as the only Ekiti town never conquered during the region's 19th-century inter-tribal wars, a claim locally attributed in part to the protection of these hill deities. The annual Olosunta festival grew out of this belief system.
