# Epa Masquerade Festival (Isan-Ekiti)

A masquerade (Epa) festival held annually in Isan-Ekiti, part of a wider Epa masking tradition found across Ekiti and neighbouring Yoruba areas, associated with large carved wooden masks.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Epa masks are among the most studied forms of Yoruba/Ekiti carving art (see the Efon-Alaaye woodcarving tradition entry); the Isan festival is one of the named occasions on the state's own festival calendar.
