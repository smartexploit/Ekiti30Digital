# Ado-Ekiti Cotton Weaving Tradition

A long-standing local tradition of cotton weaving and textile production in Ado-Ekiti, which fed into the establishment of a large textile mill in the town in 1967.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Cited by Encyclopaedia Britannica as one of Ado-Ekiti's defining traditional industries alongside shoemaking and pottery, reflecting the town's craft economy prior to and alongside its political importance as the Ewi's seat.
