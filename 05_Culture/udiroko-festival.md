# Udiroko Festival

Ado-Ekiti's new-year festival, in which the Ewi, his chiefs, and Ado indigenes at home and in the diaspora converge at the palace under the historic Iroko tree site for prayers ("Iwure"), cultural displays and a royal address.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Oral tradition dates the festival's origin to around 1310 AD, in the reign of the first Ewi, Awamaro; it is described as Ado-Ekiti's most unifying annual event after the Ogun festival. In 2024 it was attended by a United Nations World Tourism Organization delegation assessing Ekiti for a state tourism masterplan.
