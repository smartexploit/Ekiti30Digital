# Aeregbe Festival

## Documented Facts

Aeregbe is an independently-documented ancient Yoruba festival tradition, practised in various Ekiti/Yoruba towns and traditionally marked by frying bean-cake (akara) and a market closure. The Ekiti State Government's own festival calendar additionally states that a shared version of the festival is celebrated jointly across three specific Ekiti towns: Afao-Ekiti, Igbara-Odo-Ekiti and Emure-Ekiti.

## Verification Breakdown

- **Aeregbe as a genuine, ancient Ekiti/Yoruba festival tradition (general)** — *Verified* (Wiktionary + National Institute for Cultural Orientation)
- **Specifically joint between Afao-Ekiti, Igbara-Odo-Ekiti and Emure-Ekiti** — *Needs verification* (Ekiti State Government festival calendar only — no independent corroboration of this specific three-town claim)

## Notes / Uncertainties

LGA breakdown added in this revision (Afao is in Irepodun/Ifelodun LGA per the Ekiti State Government's own town-hall records; Igbara-Odo is in Ekiti South-West LGA per that LGA's official profile; Emure-Ekiti is the headquarters town of Emure LGA). The festival itself is listed only in the government's summary table with no independent account found — needs corroboration.
