# Olowe of Ise Sculpture Legacy

Olowe of Ise (d. 1934) was a master carver whose veranda posts, palace doors and bowls are considered among the finest examples of Yoruba sculpture; his works are held in museums across the US and Europe.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Widely regarded as one of the most important individually-named African sculptors of the 20th century; Ekiti State's plan to build a museum on his home compound is explicitly framed as an effort to repatriate recognition of his legacy to Ise-Ekiti.
