# Ado ni Ile Ifa (Ado-Ekiti's Ifa heritage)

Ado-Ekiti is traditionally referred to as "Ado ni ile Ifa" ("Ado, the home of Ifa"), reflecting a belief that Orunmila — the Yoruba deity associated with the Ifa divination system — resided there for an extended period.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Positions Ado-Ekiti as a significant spiritual centre within Yoruba traditional religion, alongside its political status as the Ewi's capital.
