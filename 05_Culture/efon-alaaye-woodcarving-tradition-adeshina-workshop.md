# Efon-Alaaye Woodcarving Tradition (Adeshina Workshop)

In the early 20th century, Efon-Alaaye was the leading centre of wood carving in southern Ekiti, anchored by the Adeshina family workshop, which produced palace veranda posts, doors, Ibeji (twin) figures and the carved wooden Epa masks used in local masquerades. The workshop was also known for beadwork, including beaded crowns.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

The Adeshina workshop's best-known carver, Agbonbiofe (c.1880-1945), carved over twenty veranda posts for the Alaaye's palace after an early-1900s fire, and his works (documented by scholars including William Fagg) are now held in major museum and private collections worldwide.
