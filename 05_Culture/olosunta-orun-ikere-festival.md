# Olosunta Orun Ikere Festival

## Documented Facts

An annual festival honouring Olosunta Mountain/deity, tied to Ikere-Ekiti's founding mythology, led by the Olukere of Ikere in council with local chiefs.

## Traditional / Community Account

The Olukere of Ikere, Oba Ganiyu Obasoyin, is directly quoted describing the festival as having "grown to an international level, attracting visitors from far and wide" — this is his own promotional characterisation as the festival's traditional patron, not an independently measured attendance figure.
