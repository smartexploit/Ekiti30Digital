# Ogun Onire Festival

An annual festival honouring the deity Ogun (god of iron and war), who Yoruba tradition holds was once the earthly king of Ire before disappearing into the earth ("wo ile sun") at Ire-Ekiti rather than dying.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

One of the most prominent Ogun-worship centres in Yorubaland; the festival is significant enough that a 2020 government order suspending it (a COVID-19 measure) triggered serious local unrest, underlining its centrality to Ire-Ekiti identity.
