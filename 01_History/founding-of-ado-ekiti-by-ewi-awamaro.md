# Founding of Ado-Ekiti by Ewi Awamaro

Oral tradition holds that Ado-Ekiti was founded by Ewi Awamaro, a prince originally from Ile-Ife, who migrated west (via Oba-Ile, Ido-Ani and Agbado) and settled at the site of an existing community called Ulesun/Ilesun, ruled by Elesun, which he took over and renamed Ado.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

Establishes Ado-Ekiti's status as an ancient Yoruba settlement and traces the Ewi royal lineage — now the paramount traditional authority of the state capital — back to Ile-Ife, the traditional origin point of the Yoruba people. Founding-date estimates vary by source (12th vs. 14th century) and should be treated as unresolved.
