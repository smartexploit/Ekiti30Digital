# Ikere-Ekiti's Unconquered Status in Inter-tribal Wars

Ikere-Ekiti is traditionally described as the only town in Ekiti that was never conquered during the region's 19th-century inter-tribal wars, a status locally credited in part to the protective power of the Olosunta and Orole hill deities.

## Historical / Cultural Significance

A significant point of local pride and identity for Ikere-Ekiti, tying its military history directly to its natural landscape and traditional religion.
