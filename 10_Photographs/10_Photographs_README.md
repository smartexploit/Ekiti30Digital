## File Naming

Photograph filenames use the canonical three-digit EK record ID followed by a short description.

Examples:

```text
EK-002-bawa-first-gov.jpg
EK-020-ikogosi-warm-springs.jpg
EK-039-udiroko-festival.jpg
EK-046-segun-oni-2009.jpg
```

The three-digit format is used consistently so that photographs can be linked reliably to the corresponding timeline records.

## Source, Rights and Verification Metadata

Detailed metadata for the photographs is recorded in:

`photograph_metadata_tracker.csv`

The tracker records the EK timeline ID, image description, original source, source URL, creator or owner where known, copyright/reuse-permission status, attribution requirement, verification status, reviewer field, image treatment, and research notes.

Finding or hosting an image does **not** by itself establish permission to reuse or republish it. Where copyright, licence, ownership or reuse permission has not been established, the tracker records that status as pending verification rather than treating the image as cleared for public publication.

Images should not be treated as fully verified archival assets until their provenance and applicable reuse rights have been reviewed.

## Image Treatment

The tracker also records whether an uploaded asset is an original downloaded image or has received any presentation treatment.

The Mohammed Inua Bawa photograph (`EK-002`) included in this folder is the **original downloaded image**. The AI-enhanced version produced separately during research was **not used in this collection**.

Where an image is cleaned, cropped, restored or otherwise enhanced in the future, that treatment should be recorded separately from the original source and should not be presented as an unaltered historical photograph.

## Sources

Images in this collection were gathered from traceable research sources including government and institutional websites, news publications, historical pages, social-media posts and other publicly accessible sources.

The individual source and rights information for each photograph is maintained in `photograph_metadata_tracker.csv`. Where the original creator, licence or reuse permission is unknown, this is stated in the tracker and remains subject to verification.

This photograph collection should remain consistent with the historical-media records maintained under `16_Media/Historical/`.
