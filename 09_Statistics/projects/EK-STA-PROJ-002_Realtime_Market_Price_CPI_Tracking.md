# EK-STA-PROJ-002: Multi-Market Localised Consumer Price Index (CPI) Tracking Expansion

- **Purpose:** Upgrading food and essential commodity market price sampling from Ado-Ekiti urban markets to major rural commodity trading centers across all three senatorial districts.
- **Responsible Institution:** EKSBS Division of Finance & Economic Statistics.
- **Implementation & Status:** Active project expanded in 2024; mobile handheld survey terminals deployed to price collectors in Ikole, Ijero, and Ikere markets.
- **Scope & Outputs:** Generates monthly inflation bulletins reflecting localized food security trends and rural-urban market price differentials.
