# EK-STA-PROJ-001: Property Identification, Numbering and Enumeration Exercise (EK-PINEE)

- **Purpose:** Comprehensive geospatial enumeration tagging all residential buildings, commercial structures, utilities, and roads with unique digital property identification numbers across Ekiti State.
- **Responsible Institution:** Ekiti State Geospatial Data Center (EK-SGDC) in collaboration with EKSBS.
- **Implementation & Status:** Active field project launched in September 2021; property tagging ongoing across urban centres (Ado, Ikere) and rural LGA hubs.
- **Scope & Outputs:** Provides accurate building density, address systems, and tax mapping data to support municipal service planning and internal revenue optimization.
