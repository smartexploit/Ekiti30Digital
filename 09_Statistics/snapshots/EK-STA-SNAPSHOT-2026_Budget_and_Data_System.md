# 2026 Macroeconomic Baseline & Statistical Telemetry Outlook

- **Macroeconomic Target Baseline:**
  - **Projected Population:** 3,920,000 persons (Derived from NPC 2006 baseline applying a 3.2% annual exponential growth model; *2026-2028 Medium-Term Expenditure Framework, p. 12*).
  - **Target State GDP (SGDP):** N2.40 Trillion (Nominal SGDP projection benchmarked against expanded services, agriculture value chains, and revenue telemetry; *2026 Approved State Budget Document, Schedule II*).
- **Key Data System Initiatives:**
  - Deployment of automated real-time internal revenue telemetry between the Ekiti State Internal Revenue Service (EKIRS) and EKSBS central statistical repository.
  - Institutionalization of geospatial property tagging datasets from the Property Identification, Numbering and Enumeration Exercise (EK-PINEE) to improve local government tax mapping and urban service planning.
- **Uncertainty & Data Gaps:** Projections rely on 2006 NPC census baselines pending the conduct and release of the official National Population and Housing Census.
