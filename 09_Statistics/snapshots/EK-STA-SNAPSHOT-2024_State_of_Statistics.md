# 2024 State of Statistics Sector Performance Audit

- **Sector Overview:** As of Q4 2024, the Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics (EKSBS) operated under the Ministry of Budget, Economic Planning and Performance Management. The state statistical system comprised 6 operational units within EKSBS and active data nodes across all 16 Local Government Areas (LGAs).
- **Core Infrastructure & Coverage:**
  - **Health Infrastructure Data:** 358 Primary Healthcare Facilities documented state-wide, with 103 fully functional PHCs integrated under the *Ulerawa* health intervention program (Source: *EKSBS 2024 Annual Abstract of Statistics, Section 3 - Health Statistics, pp. 42-45*).
  - **Education Infrastructure Data:** 1,020 public schools recorded across 16 LGAs (810 Primary and 210 Secondary schools) with digital enrollment tracking (Source: *Ministry of Education Annual School Census Report 2023/2024*).
  - **Price Monitoring:** Expanded price sampling routines using handheld digital terminals across market hubs in Ado-Ekiti, Ikere, Ikole, and Ijero LGAs (Source: *EKSBS Monthly CPI Bulletin, October 2024*).
- **Strategic Policy Framework:** Re-activation of the Joint Steering Committee to draft the *Ekiti State Statistical Master Plan (ESSMP 2025–2029)* in alignment with the National Strategy for the Development of Statistics (NSDS Phase III).
