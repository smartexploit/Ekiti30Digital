# EK-STA-PROG-005: LGA Statistical Decentralisation and Local Data Nodes Programme

- **Framework & Purpose:** Institutional program aimed at embedding trained statistical officers and standardized reporting templates within all 16 Local Government Area headquarters.
- **Implementing Institution:** EKSBS in coordination with the Ministry of Local Government Affairs.
- **Period & Status:** Ongoing infrastructure and human capacity expansion initiative since 2019.
- **Relevant Scope & Outputs:** Standardizing vital market price collection, primary healthcare registration, and informal business counts at the community and ward levels.
