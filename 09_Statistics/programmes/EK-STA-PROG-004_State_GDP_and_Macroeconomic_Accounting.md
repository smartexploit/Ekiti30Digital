# State GDP and Macroeconomic Accounting Framework (`EK-STA-PROG-004`)

## Programme Overview
The **State Gross Domestic Product (SGDP) & Sub-national Macroeconomic Accounting Framework** is an institutional statistical initiative executed by the **Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics (EKSBS)** in technical partnership with the **National Bureau of Statistics (NBS)**. The framework establishes localized System of National Accounts (SNA) standards to compute Ekiti State's total economic output, sector contributions, real/nominal growth rates, and per capita GDP metrics.

---

## Core Objectives & Strategic Alignment
1. **Sub-national Production Measurement:** Estimate the Gross Value Added (GVA) across agriculture, industry, and services within Ekiti State.
2. **Evidence-Based Fiscal Planning:** Provide baseline GDP data to inform the Ministry of Budget and Economic Planning in revenue forecasting, Medium-Term Expenditure Frameworks (MTEF), and tax capacity modeling.
3. **State Investment Profiling:** Generate standardized economic performance statistics for prospective private investors, bilateral development partners, and credit rating agencies.

---

## Implementation Methodology & Framework
* **SNA Compliance:** Built upon the United Nations System of National Accounts (SNA 2008) guidelines adopted by the NBS.
* **Sector Classification:** Uses the International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC Rev. 4) covering 46 activity sectors aggregated into Agriculture, Industry, and Services.
* **Data Sources:** 
  * **EKSBS Administrative Data:** Tax revenues, state public sector wage bills, local government expenditures, and trade licensing.
  * **Establishment Surveys:** Joint EKSBS–NBS state-wide enterprise surveys covering formal commercial enterprises, agribusinesses, and artisanal units.
  * **Household Surveys:** Living Standards Surveys and Household Consumption Expenditure studies for estimating informal sector contribution.

---

## Documented Research & Data Gaps (`RESEARCH_GAP`)

While the methodology follows national standards, severe archival time-series gaps remain within the public domain for Ekiti State:

* **Unbroken Annual SGDP Series:** Uninterrupted annual SGDP figures for Ekiti State spanning 1996 through 2020 are incomplete. Periodic joint recalculations were conducted by NBS for select years (e.g., 2013–2017 baseline exercises), but continuous annual publications require primary archival retrieval from EKSBS.
* **Informal Economy Estimation Gaps:** Granular quarterly GDP updates and localized informal sector capture models remain under development.
* **Primary Sourcing Requirement:** Accessing complete disaggregated sector tables (GVA breakdown per LGA and per sector) requires direct archival verification at the EKSBS headquarters or Ministry of Budget and Economic Planning, Ado-Ekiti.
