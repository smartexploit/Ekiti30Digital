# EK-STA-PROG-002: Annual Abstract of Statistics Publication Series

- **Framework & Purpose:** Statutory data publication routine compiling multi-sector administrative data to provide reliable baseline indicators for policy research, development planning, and budget formulation.
- **Implementing Institution:** Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics (EKSBS).
- **Period & Status:** Active recurrent annual publication since 1999.
- **Relevant Scope & Outputs:** Compiles yearly data on public primary/secondary school enrollment, hospital admissions, agricultural production yields, public finance revenues, and road network mileage across all 16 LGAs.
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED_OFFICIAL
