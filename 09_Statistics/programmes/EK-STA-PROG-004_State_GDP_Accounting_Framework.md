# EK-STA-PROG-004: State Gross Domestic Product (SGDP) Re-basing and Accounting Framework

- **Framework & Purpose:** Macroeconomic accounting framework designed to measure the economic output, sectoral value-add, and growth performance of Ekiti State's formal and informal sectors.
- **Implementing Institution:** EKSBS in technical collaboration with National Bureau of Statistics (NBS).
- **Period & Status:** Periodic exercise (key benchmark re-basing studies completed in 2015 and 2021).
- **Relevant Scope & Outputs:** Measures agricultural, industrial, and service sector outputs across 16 LGAs to establish nominal and real SGDP figures used in state credit ratings and MTEF planning.
