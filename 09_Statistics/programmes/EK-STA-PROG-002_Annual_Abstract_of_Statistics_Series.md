# Annual Abstract of Statistics Series (`EK-STA-PROG-002`)

## Programme Overview
The **Annual Abstract of Statistics Series** is the flagship statistical compendium published by the **Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics (EKSBS)**. It serves as the primary sub-national statistical reference document, aggregating multi-sectoral administrative statistics, demographic indicators, social sector metrics, and macroeconomic data across all 16 Local Government Areas (LGAs) in Ekiti State.

---

## Core Objectives & Strategic Alignment
1. **Centralised Data Compendium:** Consolidate administrative statistics collected from Ministries, Departments, and Agencies (MDAs), local councils, and federal institutions operating within the state.
2. **Policy Baseline & Tracking:** Serve as the authoritative baseline for state development plans, monitoring Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), and evaluating sectoral performance in education, healthcare, agriculture, and infrastructure.
3. **Public & Academic Access:** Provide validated statistical records to researchers, policy analysts, bilateral development partners, and prospective investors.

---

## Implementation Framework & Content Scope
* **Data Harmonisation:** EKSBS collates administrative records annually from line ministries (e.g., Ministry of Education, Ministry of Health, Ministry of Works) using standardized statistical reporting templates.
* **Core Statistical Domains:**
  * **Demographics & Population:** Vital registration estimates, household density, and population growth projections.
  * **Social Services:** Public/private school enrolment figures, teacher-to-student ratios, healthcare facility utilization, and safe water access metrics.
  * **Economic & Fiscal Data:** Internally Generated Revenue (IGR), statutory allocations, market price indices, agricultural production tonnage, and road infrastructure mileage.
* **Publication Mandate:** Statutory requirement to produce and distribute periodic printed and digital abstract editions to state libraries, government planning units, and public repositories.

---

## Documented Research & Data Gaps (`RESEARCH_GAP`)

While the Annual Abstract is the statutory flagship publication of EKSBS, significant publication continuity and digital archiving gaps persist:

* **Publication Intermittency:** Historical editions have experienced publication gaps across successive state administrations, resulting in non-continuous annual releases between 1996 and 2026.
* **Digital Archiving Constraints:** Pre-2010 editions remain predominantly in physical print at the EKSBS Secretariat, lacking centralized digital PDF availability on the state web portal.
* **Primary Sourcing Requirement:** Obtaining full physical copies for missing intermediate fiscal years requires direct archival verification at the EKSBS Library, State Secretariat Complex, Ado-Ekiti.
