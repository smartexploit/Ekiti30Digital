# LGA-Level Data Decentralisation (`EK-STA-PROG-005`)

## Programme Overview
The **LGA-Level Data Decentralisation Framework** is an operational policy initiative led by the **Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics (EKSBS)** in collaboration with the **Ministry of Local Government Affairs** and the **16 Local Government Areas (LGAs)** of Ekiti State. The programme aims to establish localized statistical collection units across all municipal headquarters, shifting statistical reporting from a centralized state-level model to a bottom-up, LGA-decentralized data architecture.

---

## Core Objectives & Strategic Alignment
1. **Granular Local Planning:** Enable the 16 Local Government Councils to produce localized baseline data for grassroots budgeting, primary healthcare planning, and basic education monitoring.
2. **Administrative Record Digitisation:** Modernize vital registration (births, deaths, marriages) and local business licensing registers directly at the municipal level.
3. **Statistical Capacity Building:** Deploy trained statistical officers and standardized data collection protocols across all LGA planning units to ensure consistency with state and national standards.

---

## Implementation Methodology & Framework
* **Statistical Desks at LGA Headquarters:** Institutionalizing dedicated Statistical/Planning Units within each of the 16 LGA secretariats (e.g., Ado-Ekiti, Ikole, Oye, Ijero, Gbonyin).
* **Standardised Data Collection Templates:** Standardizing monthly administrative reporting templates for market prices, agricultural yields, primary health center (PHC) attendance, and public primary school enrollment.
* **Inter-Governmental Data Transmission:** Establishing regular data aggregation channels between LGA statistical units and the central EKSBS databank in Ado-Ekiti.

---

## Documented Research & Data Gaps (`RESEARCH_GAP`)

While the policy framework mandates decentralized statistical reporting across all 16 LGAs, significant operational and data availability gaps persist:

* **Variable Infrastructure Readiness:** Hardware, stable internet access, and digital census mapping tools remain unevenly distributed across rural LGAs compared to urban centers like Ado-Ekiti.
* **Unpublished LGA Statistical Handbooks:** Historical, publication-ready annual statistical abstracts disaggregated purely at the LGA level are inconsistent across successive fiscal years.
* **Primary Sourcing Requirement:** Verifying active staffing levels, computing capacity, and real-time data submission compliance for individual LGA statistical desks requires primary field auditing across local council secretariats.
