# EK-STA-PROG-003: Ekiti State Open Data Initiative

- **Framework & Purpose:** Open governance initiative focused on publishing non-confidential public sector datasets in machine-readable formats to enhance civic transparency and public sector accountability.
- **Implementing Institution:** EKSBS in partnership with Ministry of Information and Bureau of ICT.
- **Period & Status:** Initiated in 2017; upgraded in 2023–2026.
- **Relevant Scope & Outputs:** Public access datasets covering state budget breakdowns, public procurement awards, school location coordinates, and health facility staffing levels.
