# EK-STA-INST-005: Ekiti State University (EKSU) Department of Statistics

- **Institutional Mandate:** Tertiary academic department responsible for undergraduate and postgraduate training, methodological research, and statistical consultation.
- **Establishment / Background:** Started as an option in the Department of Mathematical Sciences in 2002 and gained full department autonomy on July 15, 2016, within the Faculty of Science.
- **Role in Ekiti Statistical System:** Serves as an academic research partner and talent pipeline for EKSBS, specializing in probability, sampling survey techniques, and econometrics.
- **Data Produced & Managed:** Produces academic statistical models, sampling evaluations, econometric papers, and methodology validation studies.
