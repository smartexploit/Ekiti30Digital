# EK-STA-INST-002: Ministry of Budget, Economic Planning and Performance Management

- **Institutional Mandate:** Formulates medium-term macroeconomic frameworks, oversees capital budget allocation, superintends EKSBS operations, and coordinates state development policy targets.
- **Establishment / Background:** Created at state inception in 1996 (formerly Ministry of Finance and Economic Planning) and restructured to integrate performance management and statistical oversight.
- **Role in Ekiti Statistical System:** Translates statistical data generated by EKSBS into the Medium-Term Expenditure Framework (MTEF) and annual state budget allocations.
- **Data Produced & Managed:** Manages budget execution telemetry, state revenue/expenditure projections, MTEF target indicators, and development project monitoring metrics.
- **Relevant Programmes:** Oversees the Joint Steering Committee on the Review of the Ekiti State Statistical Master Plan (ESSMP 2025–2029).
