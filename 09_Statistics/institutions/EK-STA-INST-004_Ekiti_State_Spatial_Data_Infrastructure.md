# EK-STA-INST-004: Ekiti State Geospatial Data Center (EK-SGDC) / EK-GIS

- **Institutional Mandate:** Captures, stores, analyzes, and manages spatial data and geographic information to support urban planning, land administration, and infrastructure mapping across Ekiti State.
- **Establishment / Background:** Formally established under Executive Order 008 of 2020 and strengthened in 2021 to automate land registration, property identification, and geospatial infrastructure tracking.
- **Role in Ekiti Statistical System:** Provides spatial coordinates, GIS boundary layers, and property enumeration datasets to integrate spatial dimension into socio-economic statistics.
- **Data Produced & Managed:** Manages the Property Identification, Numbering and Enumeration Exercise (EK-PINEE) database, digital ward boundary maps, and e-C-of-O land registry records.
