# Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics (EKSBS)

- **Mandate:** Primary statutory body responsible for collecting, compiling, analyzing, and disseminating official statistics across Ekiti State MDAs and LGAs.
- **Enabling Legislation:** Ekiti State Statistical Law No. 8 of 2011 (*Ekiti State Legal Notice No. 12, Schedule 1*).
- **Institutional Structure:** Operates 6 specialized departments including Demographic & Social Statistics, Economic Statistics, and Research & Methodology (Source: *EKSBS Organizational Audit Report 2024, Section 1, pp. 3-6*).
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED_OFFICIAL
