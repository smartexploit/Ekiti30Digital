# EK-STA-INST-001: Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics (EKSBS)

- **Institutional Mandate:** Central statistical agency tasked with coordinating the State Statistical System, conducting household and economic surveys, compiling socio-economic indicators, and maintaining the State Central Data Bank.
- **Establishment / Background:** Originally established as the Statistics Department under the Ministry of Finance and Economic Development in 1997, later granted operational autonomy as a parastatal under the Ministry of Budget, Economic Planning and Performance Management via State Law No. 4 of 2009.
- **Role in Ekiti Statistical System:** Serves as the primary regulatory and coordinating authority for all statistical activities across state Ministries, Departments, and Agencies (MDAs) and Local Government Areas (LGAs).
- **Data Produced & Managed:** Produces the Annual Abstract of Statistics, monthly Consumer Price Index (CPI) bulletins, State Gross Domestic Product (SGDP) estimates, and education/health administrative data.
- **Key Departments:** Comprises six operational departments: Methodology, Evaluation & Standards; Research, Census & Surveys; Finance & Economic Statistics; Social Statistics & Population Matters; Administration & Supplies; and Finance & Accounts.
