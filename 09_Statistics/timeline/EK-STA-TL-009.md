# EK-STA-TL-009: Launch of Ekiti State Open Data Portal Initiative

**Date:** 2017-11-05  
**Primary Source:** Ministry of Information & Open Governance Initiative Report (2017)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
The state government launched the pilot Ekiti Open Data Portal to publish key sector indicators, public procurement datasets, and annual budget breakdowns in machine-readable CSV format.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
