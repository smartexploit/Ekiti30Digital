# EK-STA-TL-010: Ekiti State Statistical Master Plan (ESSMP) Launch

**Date:** 2019-05-14  
**Primary Source:** Ekiti State Statistical Master Plan Document (2019)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
EKSBS launched the 5-year Ekiti State Statistical Master Plan (ESSMP 2019–2024) aligned with the National Strategy for the Development of Statistics (NSDS) to modernize LGA data collection nodes.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
