# EK-STA-TL-015: Ekiti Data Bank Modernisation and 2026 System Launch

**Date:** 2026-02-18  
**Primary Source:** Ministry of Budget and Economic Planning Technical Release (2026)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
Unveiled the modernized Ekiti Central Data Bank, providing real-time telemetry, spatial data integration, and open statistical APIs for public policy research and economic planning.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
