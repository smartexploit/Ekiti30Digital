# EK-STA-TL-003: First Ekiti State Statistical Year Book Publication

**Date:** 1999-11-20  
**Primary Source:** Ekiti State Statistical Year Book (1999 Edition)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
The Statistics Department published the state's first comprehensive Statistical Year Book, compiling baseline school enrollment, public health facility counts, and civil service staffing across 16 LGAs.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
