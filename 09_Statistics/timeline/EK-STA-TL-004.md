# EK-STA-TL-004: National Population Census 2006 Ekiti State Breakdown

**Date:** 2006-03-21  
**Primary Source:** National Population Commission (NPC) 2006 Final Figures  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
The 2006 National Population and Housing Census recorded Ekiti State's population at 2,398,957 (1,215,487 males and 1,183,470 females), making Ado, Ikere, and Oye the most populous LGAs.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
