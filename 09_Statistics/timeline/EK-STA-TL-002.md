# EK-STA-TL-002: Establishment of Statistics Department in Ministry of Finance

**Date:** 1997-03-15  
**Primary Source:** Ekiti State Civil Service Directory (1997)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
The Ekiti State Military Administration established the Statistics Department under the Ministry of Finance and Economic Development to collect administrative records and monitor state budgetary allocations.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
