# EK-STA-TL-012: Digital Village-Level Infrastructure Mapping Project

**Date:** 2022-10-10  
**Primary Source:** EK-GIS Facility Location Database (2022)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
In collaboration with the Ekiti State Spatial Data Infrastructure Agency (EK-GIS), EKSBS mapped 1,200+ public schools, PHCs, water facilities, and agricultural processing hubs across all 177 political wards.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
