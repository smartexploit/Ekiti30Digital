# EK-STA-TL-001: State Creation Baseline Population and Asset Division

**Date:** 1996-10-01  
**Primary Source:** Federal Military Decree No. 34 (1996) / Ondo State Asset Sharing Committee Report  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
Upon creation from Ondo State on 1 October 1996, Ekiti State inherited an estimated population baseline of ~1.65 million (based on 1991 NPC projections) and 16 local government areas. Initial statistical assets were divided from the Ondo State Ministry of Finance and Planning.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
