# EK-STA-TL-013: LGA-Level Consumer Price Index (CPI) Rollout

**Date:** 2024-03-15  
**Primary Source:** EKSBS Monthly CPI Bulletin (March 2024)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
EKSBS expanded its price monitoring system from Ado-Ekiti to market centers in Ikole, Ijero, and Ikere, establishing localized CPI tracking for food and essential household commodities.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
