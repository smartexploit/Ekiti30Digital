# EK-STA-TL-008: Joint NBS-EKSBS State GDP Computation

**Date:** 2015-04-18  
**Primary Source:** NBS State GDP Computation Series (2015)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
EKSBS and NBS completed the state's first joint State Gross Domestic Product (SGDP) computation, re-basing the economy to ~N720 Billion nominal GDP with agriculture contributing over 45%.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
