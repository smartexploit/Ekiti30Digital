# EK-STA-TL-007: First Comprehensive Household Poverty and Living Standards Survey

**Date:** 2012-09-01  
**Primary Source:** NBS-EKSBS Poverty Profile Report (2012)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
EKSBS, in technical partnership with the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS), conducted the state's first dedicated household poverty and multidimensional living standards survey across all 16 LGAs.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
