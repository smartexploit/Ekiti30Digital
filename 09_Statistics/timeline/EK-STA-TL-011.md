# EK-STA-TL-011: COVID-19 Socio-Economic Impact Assessment Survey

**Date:** 2020-08-22  
**Primary Source:** EKSBS COVID-19 Impact Assessment Report (2020)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
EKSBS conducted a rapid socio-economic assessment survey measuring the impact of pandemic lockdowns on market prices, informal sector business revenues, and household income across Ekiti LGAs.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
