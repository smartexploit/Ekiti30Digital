# EK-STA-TL-005: Enactment of Ekiti State Statistical Law

**Date:** 2009-06-12  
**Primary Source:** Ekiti State Official Gazette No. 4 of 2009  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
The Ekiti State House of Assembly passed the Statistical Law (Law No. 4 of 2009), establishing the legal mandate for standardized data collection, mandatory reporting by ministries, and respondent privacy protection.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
