# EK-STA-TL-006: Autonomy and Creation of Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics

**Date:** 2011-08-10  
**Primary Source:** EKSBS Operational Blueprint (2011)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
The Statistics Department was formally transformed into an autonomous body—the Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics (EKSBS)—headed by a Statistician-General to serve as the main coordination body for the State Statistical System.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
