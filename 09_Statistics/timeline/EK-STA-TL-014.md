# EK-STA-TL-014: Automated Real-Time Revenue Data Telemetry Integration

**Date:** 2025-07-01  
**Primary Source:** EKIRS-EKSBS Data Interchange Agreement (2025)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED_RECORD  

## Record Summary
Integrated real-time data feeds between the Ekiti State Internal Revenue Service (EKIRS) and EKSBS central data repository to automate formal and informal revenue statistical reporting.

## Context & Evidence
This record represents a validated historical milestone in the 30-year evolution of Ekiti State's statistical architecture, transitioning from paper-based administrative records in 1996 to digital data telemetry in 2026.
